# Community code of conduct

SRay welcomes participation regardless of disability, hearing status, language, nationality, race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, age or experience.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific and constructive; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect Deaf communities, sign-language identities and individual communication preferences.
- Make discussions accessible and allow time for asynchronous participation.
- Protect privacy and obtain permission before sharing anyone's words, recordings or research participation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable in project issues, reviews or community activities.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/azizbek-asadovnzzemu). If no private option is available, post only a request for a private contact method, without incident details. GitHub's Report abuse controls are available for platform-policy violations, including concerns about the maintainer.

The maintainer will assess reports confidentially where possible and may request correction, remove content, restrict participation temporarily or exclude a participant. Actions should be proportionate to severity and repeated behavior. Participants may request reconsideration through the same contact route. Do not retaliate against people who report concerns.
